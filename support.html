Support

Answers first, forms second. If the FAQ doesn't cover it, the contact form at the bottom reaches a human.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly do I get when I buy a template?

An instant email with download links for every format listed on the product page (DOCX, Google Docs, Figma, LaTeX where noted), plus the writing guide for that template. Files are yours to keep and re-download anytime.

Will these templates really pass ATS scanners?

Every template is parse-tested against the five most-used applicant tracking systems before it ships. Headings, dates, titles and skills all extract cleanly — that's the parser gate, and nothing ships without passing it.

Which software do I need to edit them?

Any one of: Microsoft Word, Google Docs (free), or Figma (free tier works). Each product page lists its formats. LaTeX versions are included where academics expect them.

Can I use one template for multiple job applications?

Yes — the personal license covers unlimited applications, exports and revisions for you. It doesn't cover reselling the files or using them to make resumes for paying clients (see licensing below).

Do I get updates when a template is revised?

Yes, forever and free. When hiring norms shift and we revise a template you own, the new version lands in your inbox automatically.

I bought a bundle — how do the savings work?

Bundles are priced below the sum of their parts (the product page shows the exact saving). You receive every included template individually, each with its own updates.

Licensing

Personal license (every purchase). Use the template for your own job search: unlimited edits, exports and applications. Install the files on any devices you own.

Not included. Reselling or redistributing the files, publishing them as your own template product, or producing resumes for paying clients. If you're a career coach or agency, contact us for a commercial license — it exists and it's reasonable.

Refunds

14-day guarantee. Digital files can't be returned, but confidence matters more: if a template genuinely doesn't work for you within 14 days of purchase, write to us with a sentence about what went wrong and we'll refund it.

Found a parsing bug instead? Report it — we fix those fast, and bug reporters get the next template free.
