Frequently asked questions

What exactly do I get when I buy a product?

An instant email with download links for every format listed on the product page (PNG, SVG, AI, PSD, Figma, fonts or PDF, depending on the item), plus a license summary. Files are yours to keep and re-download anytime from your account.

Which software do I need to open the files?

It depends on the product — each listing states exactly what it needs. Vector files (AI, SVG) open in Illustrator, Figma or free tools like Inkscape; PSD needs Photoshop or a compatible editor; fonts install directly into your OS; ebooks and guides are plain PDF.

What license do I get with a purchase?

Every purchase includes a commercial-use personal license: unlimited personal and client projects, no attribution required. It doesn't cover reselling or redistributing the raw files as your own product (see licensing below).

Do I get updates if a product is revised?

Yes, forever and free. If we add formats, fix a layer issue or extend a set, the update lands in your account automatically — no repurchase.

I bought a bundle — how do the savings work?

Bundles are priced below the sum of their parts (the product page shows the exact saving). You receive every included item individually, each with its own updates.

Can I use these files for client work?

Yes. The standard license covers unlimited personal and client projects. It doesn't cover reselling the source files themselves as a competing product — see licensing below for the full boundary.

Licensing

Standard license (every purchase). Use the asset in unlimited personal and client projects — social posts, print, packaging, apps, merch, marketing. No attribution required.

Not included. Reselling or redistributing the raw source files (as-is or lightly modified) as your own downloadable product, or including them in a competing template/asset marketplace. If you need broader redistribution rights, contact us for an extended license.

Refunds

14-day guarantee. Digital files can't be "returned," but confidence matters more: if a product genuinely doesn't work for you within 14 days of purchase, write to us with a sentence about what went wrong and we'll refund it.

Found a broken file or missing layer instead? Report it — we fix those fast, and bug reporters get the next product free.
