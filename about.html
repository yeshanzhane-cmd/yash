The method

Three gates before a template ships

The parser gate

Every layout is run through the five most-used applicant tracking systems. If a single field mis-parses — a date, a title, a skill — the layout is rebuilt until it reads clean.

The recruiter gate

A panel of working recruiters skims each template the way they skim real applications. If the key facts don't surface in the first pass, it doesn't ship.

The outcome gate

We track anonymized interview-rate feedback from buyers. Templates that underperform get revised — and every revision ships to existing buyers free.
